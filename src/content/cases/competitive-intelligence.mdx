This gave the report a defined comparison basis without treating every source refresh as proof of complete or current data.'
      }
    </p>
    <h3>{'Keep summary and detail at the right level'}</h3>
    <p>
      {
        'Reporting grain describes what one record or measure represents. I kept summary measures separate from lower-level search-term data and used a product-level relationship to control how the views connected. This addressed the risk of multiplying summary values when several detail records related to one product.'
      }
    </p>
    <p>
      {

Product-comparison filters and within-product search-term ranking served different purposes: one defined the comparison group, while the other helped investigate a selected product without overloading the main report.'
